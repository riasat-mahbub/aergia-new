## Background

Drag-and-drop section reordering with @dnd-kit

`@dnd-kit/sortable`. Cross-zone section movement uses a `DragOverlay`
for visual feedback. Sub-section entries within editors also support
drag-to-reorder via a unified `DndContext`.
