# FEAT-01M2V0E529343QEA6AE2K5XPW9

## Background

Simplifying homepage content while preserving the Product Atlas visual system, real product media, feature coverage, route behavior, and section structure. Shortening repeated explanations, reducing ledger rows, and tightening headings, labels, and calls to action.

## Investigation

The expanded Product Atlas page was visually strong but repeated the same
capabilities in the hero, feature ledgers, captions, process strip, and trust
section. That repetition made the page long and made the primary story harder
to scan.

## Decision

Keep the ten-section visual structure, real product screenshots, product tour,
auth-aware actions, and distinct coverage of CV authoring, Library, applications,
tailoring, privacy, and PDF output. Simplify only the language and repeated
content so each section has one clear job.

## Implementation

Shortened the hero, premise, system map, authoring, Library, application,
tailoring, trust, tour, and final CTA copy. Reduced the authoring ledger from
four rows to three, removed the redundant Library ledger, and tightened the
application process labels and tailoring bullets.

## Verification

Passed frontend lint (nine pre-existing hook warnings), TypeScript
type-checking, production build, architecture boundary checks, and browser
rendering. At 1440px the page is 10,085px tall; at 390px it is 14,044px tall.
Both widths have no horizontal overflow, console errors, or missing images.
