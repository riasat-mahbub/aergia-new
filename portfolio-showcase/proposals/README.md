# Aergia home page directions

These are isolated, static design proposals. They do not change the live home
route. Both directions use frames from Aergia's real portfolio-showcase flow,
the fictional Maya Chen demo CV, and the existing product palette.

## Concept A — Editorial journey

`concept-editorial.html` gives the CV itself the lead role and unfolds the
product as a clear four-part journey: Begin, Shape, Reuse, Apply. It is the
calmer and more narrative direction, with the best balance between explaining
the product and showing the real interface.

## Concept B — Product atlas

`concept-gallery.html` is darker, denser, and more product-forward. Actual
screens form an image-led atlas organized by three user outcomes: Author,
Remember, and Pursue. It has more visual energy and makes the breadth of the
application immediately obvious.

## Concept B2 — Expanded product atlas

`concept-gallery-expanded.html` develops the selected Product Atlas direction
into a fuller homepage system. It keeps the dark, image-led identity while
adding a connected-product map, annotated product anatomy, detailed capability
ledgers, the complete Library taxonomy, application context and relevance,
both standard and coding-agent tailoring paths, privacy/output assurances, and
a more explicit route from source CV to reviewed PDF.

Unlike the first-direction mockup, B2 distinguishes related capabilities
inside each outcome instead of relying on screenshots alone. The page remains
persuasive rather than becoming product documentation: details are layered
under four strong chapters and actual Aergia screens still do most of the
visual work.

The accompanying `concept-gallery-expanded.md` records the page architecture,
visual system, interaction behavior, responsive rules, accessibility checks,
and the intended production adaptation.

## Workspace directions

`workspace-directions.html` extends the selected Product Atlas system into the
authenticated product and public utility pages. It compares two shared-shell
directions—Atlas Workbench and Paper Desk—then proposes a distinct treatment
for the dashboard, CV shelf, builder, Library, applications index, application
case file, settings, authentication, and agent-tailoring handoff.

The board uses the real captured screens in `assets/` as visual anchors. The
companion `workspace-directions.png` and
`workspace-directions-mobile.png` are the rendered desktop and mobile review
boards. This is an isolated proposal and does not change the live routes.
