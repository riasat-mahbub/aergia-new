Two possible design systems for the authenticated product, both grounded in the new Product Atlas home page and the real Aergia workflow. The page proposals below show what each route should feel like, what it should foreground, and which existing screens can anchor it.

Working recommendation

Use Atlas Workbench as the shared visual language: a dark navigation rail, paper work surfaces, mint state accents, editorial metadata, and fewer rounded cards. Keep the Builder intentionally denser, and let the public auth and tailoring pages borrow the home page's generous editorial composition.

00 / TWO SYSTEMS

Choose how much of the atlas should enter the product.

Both options preserve the current information architecture and behavior. They differ in shell, density, and how much of the home-page drama remains visible once the user is working.
